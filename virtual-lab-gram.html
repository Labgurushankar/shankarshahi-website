2. Principle, Reagents & Procedure

Principle

Gram-positive organisms retain the crystal violet–iodine complex after controlled decolorization and appear purple. Gram-negative organisms lose the primary stain on decolorization and take up the counterstain, appearing pink/red.

Reagents

  • Crystal violet
  • Gram iodine
  • Decolorizer
  • Safranin
  • Clean water / wash bottle

Correct order

  • Prepare thin smear
  • Heat fixation
  • Crystal violet
  • Wash
  • Gram iodine
  • Wash
  • Decolorize
  • Wash immediately
  • Safranin
  • Wash and dry
  • Microscopy
