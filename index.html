Shankar Shahi - Senior Medical Laboratory Technologist & Quality Control Expert

Mr. Shankar Shahi is a Senior Medical Laboratory Technologist and Quality Control Expert with more than 20 years of experience in the medical laboratory field and more than 5 years of focused experience in Quality Control and Quality Management Systems.

His professional interests include clinical laboratory interpretation, internal quality control, external quality assessment, method verification, laboratory standardization, SOP development, staff training, and practical implementation of quality systems in diagnostic laboratories.
