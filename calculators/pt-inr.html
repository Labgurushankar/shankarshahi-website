Enter patient PT, MNPT and reagent/instrument ISI.
Formula
INR = (Patient PT ÷ MNPT)ISI

Use the ISI assigned/validated for the thromboplastin and instrument system. MNPT is the laboratory mean normal prothrombin time determined for that method.
